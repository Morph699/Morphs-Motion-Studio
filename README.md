# Morphs Motion Studio
### Turn Still Images Into Living, Multi-Layered 3D Parallax Motion

**Morphs Motion Studio** transforms ordinary 2D photos, live camera shots, and generated artwork into dynamic, spatial 3D holographic scenes. Utilizing intelligent subject-depth segmentation and real-time gyroscopic tracking, your images break free from the screen with natural tilt-responsive parallax, floating atmospheric particles, and dynamic lighting glare.

Whether designing interactive live wallpapers, crafting eye-catching social clips, or bringing personal portraits to life, Morphs Motion Studio delivers a complete, lightweight motion design suite with zero installation and absolute privacy.

### Key Capabilities & Highlights

* **Intelligent Dual-Plane Separation:** Automatically isolates foreground subjects from backgrounds using edge and color analysis, creating instant spatial separation without complex editing software.
* **Real-Time Tilt & Touch Parallax:** Experience responsive motion driven by your device’s internal gyroscope or intuitive touch drag controls.
* **Built-in Generative Art & Camera Integration:** Synthesize new atmospheric wallpapers on demand or snap live 3D portraits instantly with your front or rear camera.
* **Dynamic Motion Pacing & Depth Tuning:** Fine-tune layer depth, camera focal zoom, and animation sway speeds ranging from subtle ambient float to high-energy motion.
* **Atmospheric Particles & Surface Glare:** Real-time particle physics and radial light sheen add dimensional texture and cinematic polish to every scene.
* **Seamless Looping Video Export:** Render smooth, looping 10-second motion videos ready for dynamic lock screens, wallpaper engines, or social sharing.
* **100% Private & Air-Gapped:** All image processing, subject segmentation, and video compositing happen entirely on your local hardware. No personal media is ever uploaded to external servers.
